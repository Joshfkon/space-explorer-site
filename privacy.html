Space Explorer: Silly Planets — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Space Explorer is a game for children. We built it so that it collects nothing.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • No accounts, ads or analytics. There is no sign-in, no advertising, and no tracking or analytics of any kind.
  • No internet use. The game does not send anything over the internet. Game progress and photos taken in the game are saved only on your device.
  • Photos. If you choose to save a photo from the game, the app asks permission to add it to your Photos library. It never reads your existing photos.
  • Purchases. The optional "Full Galaxy" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see payment details. The purchase screen is behind a parent gate.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Questions? See the support page.

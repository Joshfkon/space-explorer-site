Common questions

How do I unlock the Full Galaxy?
Fly into Jupiter, or tap the gear button at the top left. A grown-up taps the three numbers shown, in order, then taps Unlock or restore. It's a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

I bought it on another device. How do I get it back?
Tap the gear button, pass the grown-ups' check, tap Unlock or restore, then Restore purchase.

How do I turn off the voice or music?
Tap the gear button. Talking voice and Music each have an On/Off switch.

How do I start over?
Tap the gear button and choose Start over (erase progress). This erases all progress on that device.

Does the game collect any data?
No. See our privacy policy.
